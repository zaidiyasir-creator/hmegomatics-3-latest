06 — Process
Process · 01 — 04

Four phases.
One protocol.

Phase 01
Reconnaissance

Every boundary mark inspected on foot. Conditions noted, photographs filed, preliminary coordinates recorded.

Phase 02
Control

GNSS network observed in three four-hour sessions, adjusted to GDM2000. Standard deviations published.

Phase 03
Boundary

RTK GNSS at each mark, total-station traverse where canopy obstructs. Held to ±5 mm horizontal.

Phase 04
Deliverable

Sealed plan, signed report, lodgement with JUPEM. Filed against a 38-year, re-checkable archive.
